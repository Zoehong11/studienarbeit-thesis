\chapter{Methodology}
\label{chap:method}
\autoref{chap:problem-formulation} defined solving Connections as a search for the partition with the highest score, while satisfying the grouping constraint, and showed that most of the information needed to identify the correct partition has to come from a scoring function rather than from feedback. This chapter focuses on the scoring function, beginning with how to construct it and then explaining how to determine the partitions once a scoring function is given.
