This chapter applies all three to Connections. Nothing here depends on how the puzzle is actually solved. The formulation and the quantities derived from it follow from the rules of the game alone, and would hold for any solver.

\autoref{sec:problem-setting} states the rules and defines the two evaluation settings used throughout this thesis. \autoref{sec:connections-csp} expresses the puzzle as a weighted CSP. \autoref{sec:hypothesis-space} counts how many candidate solutions exist and how much uncertainty that represents. \autoref{sec:feedback-channel} does the same for the feedback the game returns, and compares the two. \autoref{sec:relation-guessing-games} notes where Connections departs from the guessing-game definition.
